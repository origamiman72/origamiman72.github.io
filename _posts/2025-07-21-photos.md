Hi! Welcome to the first post of this blog. I don't know if or when I will update this, nor whether this will have any sort of consistent subject, but I suppose we'll see.

For this first post, I'm going to try to sort out some goals I have as I try to improve my photography. Generally when I take photos, it's because I see something particularly cool or notable (e.g., the Golden Gate Bridge or the downtown skyline). If nothing like that comes up then I often end up with very few images or don't use my camera at all. Clearly, this isn't a great way to take a lot of photos, which I need to do if I want to improve my skills as a photographer. 

Therefore, my goal for the next few months is to shoot more ordinary things and try to find whatever interesting views I can there. I personally enjoy looking at street photos that have a "slice-of-life" type look — I think keeping those in mind will serve as a good guideline when I go out to take photos.

One concrete way I plan to follow this is to just shoot _more_. I will carry my camera with me to more places and try to take more pictures of anything and everything. Over time I'll learn which kinds of shots I like and don't like and can shoot more intentionally then.

On a related note, my editing style tends to be quite heavy-handed; another goal of mine in these coming months is to improve my skills with more subtle editing styles. Other than the new technical skills I'll have to learn, this will also require me to put more thought into my compositions since I won't have heavy editing to fall back on to make a photo interesting. As stated previously, I think this will happen over time as I just shoot more but will also require me to critique my photos more intentionally to really figure out what I do and don't like. For now, I'll try to just shoot more and refine this editing skill more slowly over time. Hopefully in the next post (if there is one) I can report on where this journey takes me.
